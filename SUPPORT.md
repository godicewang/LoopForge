# Support

- **Bug or regression:** open a bug report with the LoopForge version, macOS
  version, reproduction steps, expected result, and sanitized logs.
- **Feature or workflow proposal:** open a feature request and describe the
  outcome rather than a specific implementation.
- **Security issue:** do not open a public issue. Follow
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

LoopForge is a community project. There is currently no guaranteed response
time or commercial support commitment.
