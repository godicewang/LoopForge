# Code of Conduct

LoopForge is built by people with different backgrounds and experience. We are
committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing evidence and technical trade-offs.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and help repair harm.
- Respect privacy. Never publish another person's credentials, task output, or
  identifying information.

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized conduct, threats, deliberate disruption,
and publishing private information are not tolerated.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct
issues privately through the repository's **Report abuse** flow or a private
security advisory when confidentiality is required. Maintainers will review
reports promptly, protect reporters' privacy, and respond proportionately.

This policy applies in project spaces and whenever someone officially
represents the project.
